Revisiting ELT Fundamentals

Revisiting ELT Fundamentals • By Fajarudin Akbar

As educational technology advances rapidly and artificial intelligence tools redefine classroom prep, grounding teaching practice in core English Language Teaching (ELT) fundamentals remains paramount. Without strong pedagogical rationale, even the most sophisticated digital tools risk becoming superficial novelty.

Core Pillar: Communicative Purpose

Language acquisition thrives when tasks reflect authentic, real-world communication. Learner output should be driven by meaningful goals rather than mechanical repetition.

Key Methodological Principles

  • Learner-Centered Design: Prioritizing student talking time, cooperative problem-solving, and personalized task choices over teacher-fronted lecture.
  • Authentic Materials & Contexts: Integrating real-world text types, podcasts, and digital artifacts that resonate with students' professional and academic aspirations.
  • Balanced Focus on Form & Fluency: Developing communicative confidence alongside linguistic precision through timely, constructive feedback loops.

Connecting Theory to EdTech

When evaluated through foundational ELT theories—such as Vygotsky’s Zone of Proximal Development and Krashen’s Input Hypothesis—EdTech tools can be purposefully selected to scaffold input, lower affective filters, and elevate student autonomy.
