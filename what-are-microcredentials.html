What Are Microcredentials?

Many educators are familiar with the free, U.S. government-funded online courses for English teachers worldwide. These self-paced programs are a powerful example of microcredentials.

A microcredential is a targeted, evidence-based certification that demonstrates mastery of a specific skill, tool, or pedagogical technique. Unlike multi-year university degrees, microcredentials focus on targeted competencies that can be immediately applied in real-world teaching settings.

Real-World Example: OPEN Program

Through programs like the Online Professional English Network (OPEN) sponsored by the U.S. Department of State, thousands of English language educators globally earn digital badges and certificates in areas such as:

  • Using Educational Technology in the English Language Classroom (AE E-Teacher)
  • Teaching English to Young Learners (TEYL)
  • Fostering Student Motivation and Communicative Fluency

Unlocking Career Opportunity

The true power of microcredentials lies in the opportunity to continuously upgrade skill sets, gain global recognition, network with international peers, and showcase actionable proof of professional growth.
