Presentation Summary

1

Learners, Not Tools

Generative AI gives English teachers new possibilities for creating materials and designing learning experiences. However, effective use starts with learners, learning objectives, and teaching context, not with the selection of an AI tool.

2

Directed Workflow

This practical session presents a workflow for Teaching English with AI through two purposes: creating materials with AI and integrating AI directly into lessons for students to use.

Tools explored: ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Studio, and NoteLab English.

3

The Art of Prompting & Ethics

Participants begin with The Art of Prompting to experience communicating ideas precisely. Ethical considerations—learner age, privacy, safeguarding, accuracy, bias, and learning integrity—are integrated directly into the workflow.

The Workflow

1

The Art of Prompting

Say What You See Task + Context + Outcome

Participants experience prompting through Say What You See , describing AI-generated images with increasing precision. The activity demonstrates that communicating an idea requires more than identifying the subject. Participants need to communicate relevant details about the image, including its medium, subject, and context.

The experience leads to the question:
How clearly can we communicate what we want?

Participants then apply:

  • Task: What do I want the AI to do?
  • Context: What does it need to know?
  • Outcome: What should the result achieve?

The activity can also be transferred directly into English teaching, particularly as a writing or descriptive language activity.

2

Define Learner Needs

Learners + Level + Objective + Context

Teachers identify who their learners are, what they need to achieve, and what is currently missing from the learning experience.

The GSE Teacher Toolkit provides a practical reference for defining learner needs, proficiency, skills, and learning objectives.

3

Decide the Purpose

Create

Teacher + AI

Use AI to create, adapt, differentiate, or extend materials for learners.

Integrate

Teacher + Learners + AI

Design a learning activity in which students interact directly with AI for a specific purpose.

4

Choose the Tool

The tool follows the teaching purpose. Participants explore different possibilities for content creation, interactive tools, practice, multimodal materials, and specialised teacher resources.

ChatGPT Gemini Copilot Google AI Studio NoteLab English
5

Execute The Pathway

5A

Create Materials

Prompt Generate Review Adapt Teach

Teachers use AI to address a specific material need, then review and adapt the output before using it with learners.

5B

Integrate AI with Learners

Prepare Think Interact Evaluate Revise / Reflect

Students first engage with the learning task themselves. They then interact with AI for a defined purpose and evaluate what it produces. Examples from lesson plans include:

AI Debate Partner

Students develop their own position, use AI to generate an opposing argument, debate it, and reflect on the interaction.

AI Peer Review Table

Students write first, examine AI feedback, decide what to accept or reject, justify their decisions, and revise their writing.

AI Listening Partner

Students develop their own interpretation of a speaker's attitude, compare it with AI's interpretation, and evaluate the evidence.

AI Comprehension

Students interpret a text first, then use AI to evaluate and challenge their interpretation.

6

Ethical Check

Age Privacy Safeguarding Accuracy Bias Learning Integrity

Teachers consider whether AI use is appropriate for these particular learners and this particular activity. For student-facing AI use, this includes preparing students, testing prompts, monitoring interactions, protecting personal information, and ensuring that AI does not replace the learning process.

7

Teacher Review

Evaluate Adapt Monitor

Teachers review generated materials and monitor student interaction with AI. They check accuracy, language level, relevance, appropriateness, and alignment with the learning objective.

8

Learning Experience

What will learners actually do?

The final question is not:
What did AI generate?

It is:
What will learners do with it?

Learners should have opportunities to communicate, think, evaluate, create, revise, and make decisions.

Core Message

01 Start with learners.
02 Define the purpose.
03 Choose the resource.
04 Create or integrate.
05 Evaluate critically.
06 Design the learning experience.

"The goal is not simply to use AI in English teaching. It is to make deliberate decisions about how teachers and learners can use AI for meaningful language learning."
