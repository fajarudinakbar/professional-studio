Executive Summary

01

Learners, Not Tools

Effective AI use starts with learners, objectives, and teaching context—not the app. Technology must follow pedagogical intent.

02

Directed Workflow

There are two primary paths to integrating AI in the classroom:

Create

Teachers prep, adapt, and extend materials. Learners never touch the AI.

Integrate

Teachers design tasks where learners interact directly with AI.

03

Prompting & Ethics

Clear prompts yield better materials. Ethical considerations (age, privacy, accuracy, bias) must be embedded in every step.
